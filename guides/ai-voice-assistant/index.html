Talk with an AI assistant about what’s playing on your Mac

Ask an AI assistant about a video, demo, or other audio without having to describe the clip first.

Who it’s for

If you want to pause a clip and ask about something you just heard, let the assistant hear both the clip and your question.

What you need

A Mac running macOS 14.2 or later, Open Mic, a physical microphone, and an AI assistant with an audio input that can use Open Mic. Both audio sources reached Claude in testing.

Open Mic setup

Set it up

  1. Start the video or other audio in its own app or browser.
  2. Turn on Open Mic in the menu bar. In the assistant’s audio input settings, select Open Mic.
  3. Play a few seconds, then pause the clip and ask a question about a specific detail. Check that the assistant picked up both the clip and your question before continuing.

Turn Open Mic off when you’re done.

Tips and troubleshooting

If an input is missing, check the Open Mic menu and macOS Privacy & Security settings for microphone and system audio permission (which may be listed under Screen Recording). The left meter shows your mic; the right shows the Mac’s audio.

If the receiving app suppresses playback through echo cancellation, try using different apps or browsers for playback and capture. Open Mic cannot turn off that processing. Use headphones to keep speaker playback out of the physical mic.

If the assistant speaks back, pause or mute its input while the reply plays so the reply is not fed back into the same session.

Limits

ChatGPT accepted Open Mic and transcribed speech in testing, but the Mac’s audio did not come through. That report does not establish the cause or guarantee the same result in every version. Open Mic sends audio only; it does not share the screen, interpret a clip, or provide the assistant’s replies.
