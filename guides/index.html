Open Mic guides

Five ways to use your microphone and your Mac’s audio as one input.

  • Talk with an AI assistant about what’s playing on your Mac

    Ask an AI assistant about a video, demo, or other audio without having to describe the clip first.

  • Capture both sides of a meeting for notes

    Send your voice and the people you hear on a call to a separate app for notes or transcription.

  • Record a tutorial with narration and app sound

    Use QuickTime Player to record your screen with both your narration and the sounds from your demo.

  • Practice a language with an AI tutor and a clip

    Let an AI tutor hear a short video or podcast excerpt, then ask about a phrase or try saying it yourself.

  • Record a remote interview on one audio track

    Record your voice and a remote guest’s voice together in QuickTime Player.
