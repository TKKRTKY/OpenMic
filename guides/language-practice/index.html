Practice a language with an AI tutor and a clip

Let an AI tutor hear a short video or podcast excerpt, then ask about a phrase or try saying it yourself.

Who it’s for

For learners who want to ask about a phrase as spoken, or say it aloud while the clip is still fresh.

What you need

A Mac running macOS 14.2 or later, Open Mic, a physical microphone, a video or podcast player, and an AI tutor that accepts Open Mic as its audio input. Check that the tutor can work in the language you’re learning.

Open Mic setup

Set it up

  1. Open a short clip in a different app or browser from the tutor.
  2. Turn on Open Mic in the menu bar and choose Open Mic as the tutor’s microphone input.
  3. Play a short excerpt and pause it. Ask what a phrase means, or repeat it out loud and ask for feedback. Start with a phrase you know to check that the tutor can hear both sources.

Turn Open Mic off when you’re done.

Tips and troubleshooting

If an input is missing, check the Open Mic menu and macOS Privacy & Security settings for microphone and system audio permission (which may be listed under Screen Recording). The left meter shows your mic; the right shows the Mac’s audio.

If the receiving app suppresses playback through echo cancellation, try using different apps or browsers for playback and capture. Open Mic cannot turn off that processing. Use headphones to keep speaker playback out of the physical mic.

Use short clips at a comfortable volume rather than trying to speak over the playback. If the tutor gives a spoken reply, pause its input while it speaks so it does not hear its own reply.

Limits

Open Mic is not a tutor and doesn’t score pronunciation or translate. The tutor may mishear a phrase, especially when voices overlap. Use audio you’re allowed to share with the service and check its handling of your voice and the clip.
